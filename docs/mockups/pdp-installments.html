Before this ships

Four things about the money, not the type.

§

The “from $X/month” half is the risky half

In the Klarna reference both clauses are true of one provider: Klarna's monthly financing and its Pay-in-4 are both 0% on eligible orders. Shop Pay splits differently — the 4-payment plan is interest-free, but its monthly plans are interest-bearing and carry an order minimum. Written as one sentence, “at 0% interest” would land on both halves and be wrong about the first.

So the toggle above is off by default. If we do want the monthly clause, it needs its own rate and its own minimum: “From $12/month with Shop Pay financing, or 4 payments of $34.25 at 0% interest.”

§

Writing our own copy makes it our disclosure

Shopify ships {{ form | payment_terms }} as a locked web component partly because instalment messaging is regulated in the US. Replacing it with our own sentence means Foreign Resource is authoring that line. Keep the figure exact, point Shop Pay at Shopify's own terms page, and don't make claims Shop Pay doesn't.

§

Gate it on eligibility, not just on a checkbox

Shop Pay Installments only applies inside an eligible order-value range and for US buyers. The line renders today whenever the section setting is on, so a $28 tee and an overseas visitor both get offered something checkout will not honour. Confirm the current thresholds in the Shop Pay dashboard; the code below gates on a min and max you set in the customiser.

§

Our quarter is an even quarter; Shopify's is not

The theme divides the variant price by four. Shop Pay absorbs the odd cents into the first payment, so on a price that doesn't divide cleanly our figure can sit a penny under the one the customer actually sees. “4 payments from $34.25” is the honest fix if we don't want to replicate their rounding.
