What each one costs you

All three drop the grey pill, the 14px type and the Shop Pay lockup from the buy column. They differ in how much weight the line carries.

Before this ships

Four things that are about the money, not the type.

§

Writing our own copy makes it our disclosure

Shopify ships {{ form | payment_terms }} as a locked web component partly because instalment messaging is regulated in the US. Replacing it with our own wording means Foreign Resource is authoring that line. Keep the figure exact, keep the link pointing at Shopify's own terms, and don't add claims Shop Pay doesn't make.

§

Gate it on eligibility, not just on a checkbox

Shop Pay Installments only applies inside an eligible order-value range and for US buyers. The line renders today whenever the section setting is on, so a $28 tee and an overseas visitor both get offered something checkout will not honour. Confirm the current thresholds in the Shop Pay dashboard and gate on price and market.

§

Our quarter is an even quarter; Shopify's is not

The theme divides the variant price by four. Shop Pay absorbs the odd cents into the first payment, so on a price that doesn't divide cleanly our figure can sit a penny under the one the customer actually sees. "Payments from $34.25" is the honest fix if we don't want to replicate their rounding.

§

The Shop mark goes, unlike on Promise

The restyled Promise box keeps Shopify's mark because the programme expects it to stay recognisable. Here the mark is dropped from the bar and named in the aside instead ("At checkout with Shop Pay"), which keeps the attribution without putting a purple lockup in a column that has no other colour in it.
